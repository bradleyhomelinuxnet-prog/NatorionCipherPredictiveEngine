Your dates

Years before 1 CE are BC. Month and day are optional; with them you get the sky over that day. Dates before 15 Oct 1582 are read as Julian, as historical sources give them.

What turns up

The chronology, its ledger and its cycles are Jason Breshears' (Archaix). Addenda are their authors' own readings, credited on each. The sky comes from Astronomy Engine, the library the original v12 used, at 0h UT as printed ephemerides give it; the Moon moves about 13° a day. Most of the ledger sits on regular cycles (138-year Phoenix nodes, 600-year NER nodes, baktuns), so equally spaced triples of ledger events are built into it and are not independent evidence; the chance figures here count gaps, not triples, for that reason. This is a pattern tool for study.
